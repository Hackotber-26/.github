Hacktoberfest Hack Day Bengaluru is a one-day, in-person hackathon organized by the AWS Student Builder Group at RV University, in collaboration with ECell RV University, bringing together student developers to tackle real-world challenges through technology and innovation.

Scheduled for 31 October 2026 at RV University, Bengaluru, the event brings together shortlisted teams for an intensive day of problem-solving, collaboration, and pitching. Participants will work on challenges across Cloud Computing and Social Impact, developing practical solutions that address real-world problems.

The event features an offline hackathon followed by project presentations and final judging by an external panel of industry professionals. It provides students with an opportunity to showcase their technical skills, collaborate with fellow developers, and gain exposure to industry perspectives.

With exciting cash prizes, goodies, and opportunities to connect with the developer community, Hacktoberfest Hack Day aims to encourage innovation, hands-on learning, and a stronger student-led tech ecosystem at RV University.
